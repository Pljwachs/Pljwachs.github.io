About

I am passionate about drones and robotics, with a particular interest in multi-agent autonomy and exploration.

I believe that effective multi-agent coordination is not solely about sophisticated learning models or optimization methods, but about building systems that are stable, predictable, and reliable. My goal is to develop autonomous systems that enable teams of drones to operate reliably in challenging real-world missions, such as search-and-rescue operations.

I am currently writing my master’s thesis at the Chair of Autonomous Aerial Systems at the Technical University of Munich, where I develop algorithm for multi-agent exploration in large-scale under-canopy environments.
